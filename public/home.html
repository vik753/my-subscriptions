My Subscriptions

Track prepaid class passes — gym, lessons, any hobby.

Open the app

What it does

  • Shows how many paid sessions are left on each pass and when to renew.
  • Asks after each session whether you attended; moves the payment on if it didn't happen.
  • Lets you move or cancel sessions and correct payments.
  • Works offline as an app on your phone's Home Screen (no app store needed).

Your data

By default everything stays on your device — no account needed. For each hobby you can optionally put its sessions into a separate “My Subscriptions” Google Calendar (and share them with guests), and back it up to the app's hidden Google Drive folder. The app has no server; the author never sees your data. Details: Privacy Policy.
