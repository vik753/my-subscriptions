Privacy Policy

My Subscriptions · Effective 26 September 2026

My Subscriptions (“the app”, vik753.github.io/my-subscriptions) helps you track prepaid class passes: how many paid sessions are left, which sessions you attended, and when to renew. It is made by Ihor Korenets (idea: Netrebko Olena). This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Summary

  • The app has no server. It runs entirely in your browser.
  • By default your data is stored only on your device.
  • Google Calendar and Google Drive are optional, chosen per hobby. Only then does the app ask you to sign in with Google.
  • The author never receives, sees, stores or shares your data. There are no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your hobbies (name, schedule, sessions, attendance marks, payments), the app settings (language, theme, color scheme, reminder time) and the sync state are stored in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB). They stay on your device until you delete them.

Google user data (only if you turn it on)

For a hobby you can switch on Add to Google Calendar and/or Back up to Google Drive. The app then asks you to sign in with Google and requests exactly these permissions:

  • Google account basic info (openid, email, profile): your name and email are shown in the app's Settings, and the email is used to sign you back in.
  • Calendars created by the app (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.app.created): the app creates one secondary calendar named “My Subscriptions” and writes one event per session of the hobbies you chose (date, time, duration, status such as paid or unpaid). If you add guests to a hobby, their email addresses are added as event attendees so the sessions appear in their calendars. The app cannot see or change any of your other calendars.
  • The app's own hidden Google Drive folder (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata): the app saves one file, state.json, with the hobbies you chose to back up and your settings, so you can restore them or use several devices. The app cannot see or change any of your other files.

Google data is exchanged directly between your browser and Google. The access token is kept only for the current browser session. Nothing is sent to the author or to any third party.

Limited Use. My Subscriptions' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to provide the features described above, is never used for advertising, is never sold, and is never read by a human.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Delete data… → Delete everything removes the data on your device, the “My Subscriptions” calendar and the Drive backup; Delete only from Google removes just the calendar and the backup.
  • Switching a hobby's Google options off removes its events or its backup.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Removing the app from your device deletes the locally stored data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
